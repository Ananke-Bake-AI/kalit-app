# Kalit AI Launch Plan

## Core Positioning

Kalit should launch as an AI execution team for startup launch, not as a generic AI builder.

Best short positioning:

- Kalit helps founders launch a product end-to-end with one AI execution team.

Best expanded positioning:

- Kalit turns a startup idea into a live product, launch site, first growth system, and pre-launch security check through specialized AI suites.

## Product Story

Use the suites as one connected journey:

1. Flow
   Build the launch site and landing pages fast.
2. Project
   Build the actual product or MVP.
3. Marketing
   Generate and operate the first acquisition workflows.
4. Pentest
   Scan the product before launch and after major releases.

Do not present all four as equally important on day 1.

Day-1 commercial wedge:

- Primary: Flow + Project
- Secondary: Marketing as add-on
- Controlled beta: Pentest

## Day-1 Offer

Launch with one clear flagship offer:

- Launch Sprint

Suggested promise:

- From idea to live startup presence in 7 to 14 days.

Suggested deliverables:

- 1 launch landing page
- 1 MVP or clickable product
- 1 analytics + lead capture setup
- 1 starter campaign plan
- 1 pre-launch security scan summary

Suggested customer types:

- Pre-seed founders
- Solo founders
- Startup studios
- Agencies shipping MVPs for clients

## Packaging Recommendation

Keep pricing simple at launch.

Do not lead with four different suite pricing tables plus umbrella credits.

Suggested packaging:

1. Free
   Explore Flow, test prompts, limited generations.
2. Launch
   For founders getting a site or MVP live.
3. Growth
   Adds collaboration, deployment, and Marketing.
4. Enterprise
   Custom onboarding, Pentest, advanced security, custom infra.

Suggested rule:

- Keep usage abstraction internal at first.
- Sell outcomes first, credits second.

## Messaging Recommendation

Homepage headline options:

1. Launch your startup with one AI execution team
2. From idea to live product, site, growth, and security
3. Build, launch, grow, and secure your startup with AI

Subheadline option:

- Kalit connects specialized AI suites for product building, landing pages, growth operations, and security validation so founders can launch faster with less operational drag.

Proof points to emphasize:

- Multi-agent execution
- Prompt to deployed output
- Repo import and real project work
- Growth workspace and campaign execution
- Workspace-based security scans and reporting

## 30-Day Launch Plan

### Week 1: Focus and Packaging

Goals:

- Decide the launch wedge
- Simplify the story
- Remove pricing confusion

Actions:

1. Pick one category statement for all public surfaces.
2. Choose the primary CTA:
   - "Start your launch sprint"
   - or "Build your startup"
3. Unify pricing across landing, suite pages, and billing copy.
4. Mark Marketing and Pentest as:
   - "Included in Growth"
   - or "Beta / Expert Mode"
5. Define 3 ideal customer profiles.

Deliverables:

- Final homepage copy
- Final pricing table
- Final CTA structure
- ICP one-pager

### Week 2: Launch Readiness

Goals:

- Make the first-run experience trustworthy and measurable

Actions:

1. Instrument funnel events:
   - landing visit
   - prompt submitted
   - signup started
   - signup completed
   - first session created
   - first project created
   - first successful output
   - upgrade clicked
   - demo requested
2. Tighten onboarding for Flow and Project.
3. Add trust surfaces:
   - security page
   - privacy page
   - terms
   - responsible use / acceptable use
   - contact / support response expectations
4. Prepare launch-support workflows:
   - bug triage
   - refund policy
   - incident response
   - user feedback intake
5. Prepare internal QA checklist for the top 10 demo paths.

Deliverables:

- Launch QA checklist
- Event taxonomy
- Trust page checklist
- Support SOP

### Week 3: Proof and Customer Development

Goals:

- Collect evidence that Kalit produces business outcomes

Actions:

1. Recruit 5 to 10 design partners.
2. Run each through a guided launch sprint.
3. Capture:
   - before state
   - prompt used
   - time to first output
   - time to usable result
   - deployment result
   - measurable business outcome if possible
4. Turn the best 3 into case studies.
5. Collect testimonials and screenshots.

Deliverables:

- 3 case studies
- 5 testimonials
- demo assets
- launch screenshots

### Week 4: Public Launch

Goals:

- Release with controlled attention and fast feedback loops

Actions:

1. Publish the updated site and pricing.
2. Launch to:
   - X
   - LinkedIn
   - founder communities
   - personal network
   - waitlist / email list
3. Run founder-led outreach to the first 50 target accounts.
4. Offer white-glove onboarding calls to the first cohort.
5. Review conversion and activation daily.

Deliverables:

- public launch assets
- founder outreach list
- onboarding calendar
- daily KPI dashboard

## Metrics That Matter

Track these from day 1:

- Visitor to signup conversion
- Signup to first session conversion
- First session to first successful output conversion
- First successful output to activation conversion
- Activation to paid conversion
- Time to first value
- Weekly active workspaces
- Retained teams after 2 weeks
- Support tickets per active workspace
- Failure rate on top launch paths

Suggested activation definition:

- User creates a real project or landing output and reaches a usable result in the same day.

## Biggest Risks

### 1. Positioning dilution

Risk:

- Kalit sounds like too many products at once.

Mitigation:

- Lead with one story: launch your startup end-to-end.

### 2. Pricing confusion

Risk:

- Different suite pages imply different business models.

Mitigation:

- One pricing logic across the site.

### 3. Trust gap

Risk:

- Pentest especially raises scrutiny around safety, legal use, and reliability.

Mitigation:

- Keep Pentest in controlled beta unless trust and controls are fully packaged.

### 4. Activation gap

Risk:

- Users love the concept but do not reach a successful first output.

Mitigation:

- White-glove onboarding, demo prompts, templates, guided flows.

## Recommended Immediate Decisions

Decide these before launch:

1. Is Kalit launching as a startup launch platform or as a suite marketplace?
2. Is Pentest public or beta-only?
3. Is pricing credits-first or outcome-first?
4. Is the main CTA self-serve or book-a-demo?
5. Which single ICP gets the first 20-customer push?
